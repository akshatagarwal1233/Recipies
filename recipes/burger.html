Description:-

A hamburger, often known as a burger, consists of fillings—usually a patty of panfried or grilled ground meat, typically beef—placed inside a sliced bun, sesame seed bun, or bread roll. These patties are often served with lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, bacon, or chilis, together in the bun or roll.

Ingredients:-

  • Buns
  • Patties(vegetable,chicken or meat)
  • Cheese Slice
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Ketchup
  • Butter
  • Salt>

Steps:-

  1. Wash and slice the tomato, onion, and lettuce.
  2. Cut the burger bun in half. Apply a little butter to the cut sides and toast them on a pan until lightly golden.
  3. Mix mayonnaise and ketchup together. You can add a little mustard if you like.
  4. Assemble the burger:-
    • Place the bottom bun on a plate.
    • Spread some sauce.
    • Add lettuce
    • Place the cooked patty with melted cheese.
    • Add tomato and onion.
    • Spread more sauce.
    • Place the top bun.
  5. Gently press the burger together and serve while warm.
